# 5S Software Company Profile & Brochure Copywriting Skill

This skill provides the domain context, brand identity, service pillars, target personas, and copy templates specifically for authoring **5S Software** marketing collateral and brochures.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

*   **Company Name:** Công ty Cổ phần Phần mềm 5S (5S Software JSC)
*   **Tagline:** *Kiến tạo nền tảng phần mềm đột phá cho doanh nghiệp* (Engineering Breakthrough Software Platforms for Enterprises)
*   **Brand Essence:** Digital transformation inspired by the Japanese 5S methodology — turning *Seiri, Seiton, Seiso, Seiketsu, Shitsuke* into software excellence: **Tối ưu – Chuẩn hoá – Tinh gọn – An toàn – Bền vững**.
*   **Positioning:** B2B Enterprise Software Development & Cloud Transformation Partner, delivering custom enterprise applications (ERP, CRM, DMS, HRM), modern cloud infrastructure, and AI-powered automation.

---

## 2. Core Service Pillars & Offerings

When creating brochure panels or marketing copy for 5S Software, map capabilities into these three core pillars:

### Pillar 1: Custom Enterprise Software (Phát Triển Phần Mềm May Đo)
*   **Scope:** Tailored ERP, CRM, DMS, HRM, and logistics management systems built strictly around client workflows.
*   **Key Advantage:** 100% fit for specific industry operations; no unnecessary bloat; agile sprint-based delivery every 2 weeks.
*   **Tech Stack:** React, Next.js, Node.js (NestJS), Golang, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB.

### Pillar 2: Cloud Modernization & DevOps (Chuyển Đổi Đám Mây & DevOps)
*   **Scope:** Cloud migration (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), microservices architecture refactoring, container orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes), automated CI/CD pipelines.
*   **Key Advantage:** 99.9% uptime SLA, auto-scaling during traffic spikes, zero-downtime deployments.

### Pillar 3: AI Automation & BI Analytics (Tự Động Hóa & Báo Cáo Thông Minh)
*   **Scope:** Real-time business intelligence dashboards, automated reporting, OCR data extraction, predictive analytics.
*   **Key Advantage:** Reduces reporting latency from days to sub-second real-time dashboards; eliminates 80%+ manual data entry.
