# Brochure Software A4 Design & Pre-press Standard

Standard operating procedure and architectural guidelines for designing, copywriting, structuring, and producing print-ready A4 brochures (Bi-fold, Tri-fold, 1-page Flyer, Booklet) for B2B software products and IT services.

---

## 1. Content Strategy & Value Proposition Formula

A software brochure is a high-impact sales enablement tool designed to communicate the core value proposition in 3–5 seconds and guide the reader to a clear Call-To-Action (CTA).

### 1.1 The B2B Software Narrative Arc
Every software brochure MUST follow the **Hook → Pain → Solution → Impact → Proof → Action** sequence:
1. **Hook (Cover / Headline):** Bold value statement focusing on customer outcome.
2. **Pain Points:** 3–4 acute business challenges (cost leakage, slow turnaround, errors, security risks).
3. **Solution & Core Modules:** Highlight 3–4 signature capabilities with visual clarity.
4. **Quantified Value & ROI:** Concrete metrics (-40% costs, 3x speed, 99.9% uptime).
5. **Social Proof & Credibility:** Logos, certifications (ISO 27001), customer testimonials.
6. **Next Step (CTA):** Clear contact route (Website QR code, phone, demo link, email).

---

## 2. A4 Physical Geometry & Panel Layout Architecture

All dimensions follow ISO 216 standard A4 ($210 \times 297$ mm).

### 2.1 Format Types & Panel Logic

#### A. Tri-Fold A4 (Landscape Sheet: $297 \times 210$ mm folded into 3 panels)
*   **Outside Spread:** Flap ($97 \times 210$ mm), Back Cover ($100 \times 210$ mm), Front Cover ($100 \times 210$ mm).
*   **Inside Spread:** Left Panel ($100 \times 210$ mm), Center Panel ($100 \times 210$ mm), Right Panel ($97 \times 210$ mm).

#### B. Bi-Fold A4 (4-Page A4 / A3 Folded in half)
*   **Page 1 (Front Cover):** Brand Identity, Hero Title, Subheading, Hero Product UI.
*   **Page 2 (Inside Left):** Pain Points & Solution Overview.
*   **Page 3 (Inside Right):** Key Feature Modules & Quantified ROI.
*   **Page 4 (Back Cover):** Implementation Process, Compliance, Company Profile, CTA & Contacts.

---

## 3. Pre-press & Production Quality Control

1. **Bleed:** $3\text{ mm}$ on all outer trimming edges ($216 \times 303\text{ mm}$ canvas for single A4).
2. **Safe Zone:** Minimum $5\text{ mm}$ (recommended $8\text{–}10\text{ mm}$) inside cut lines.
3. **Color Space:** CMYK for commercial print; RGB reserved for digital interactive PDF.
4. **Resolution:** Raster images and screenshots $\ge 300\text{ DPI}$.
5. **Vector Assets:** Logos, icons, and diagrams must be SVG / vector curves.
6. **Export Format:** PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 with embedded fonts.
